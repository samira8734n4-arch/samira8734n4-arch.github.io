### Do not publish

- **The street address.** A home address on a public portfolio is a personal
  safety exposure, and it serves no recruiter need — "Dhaka, Bangladesh" answers
  the location question completely. This is standard on a CV and wrong on a
  website; the two documents have different threat models.

  recruiter who wants you will email. Recommendation: email as the public
  contact route, phone reserved for the CV file you hand over deliberately.

### Why these three

They cover three different, non-overlapping proof points rather than three
samples of the same skill:

1. **LD-Tax** — scale and stakes. A government tax system used by citizens who
   cannot opt out. Proves the ability to make genuinely hard, high-consequence
   domains usable. Nothing else in the portfolio matches its scope.
2. **PropSoft.ai** — end-to-end product ownership across three surfaces (system,
   app, marketing site). Closest analogue to a modern product design role, and
   the one that reads most legibly to a commercial product company.
3. **Nexis HRM / School Management** — systems maturity. Data-dense dashboards
   plus reusable component frameworks. This is the case study that proves
   seniority rather than output, and it is the one a design lead will value most.

## 4. Positioning

The CV's own objective statement ("Innovative Product Designer committed to
crafting user-centered experiences through research, creativity, and smart
design") is unusable on the site. It is true of every designer alive, so it
carries no information, and it directly violates the brief's tone requirement
that specificity produces confidence.

**The actual differentiator, which the CV buries:** almost the entire portfolio
is complex enterprise and government software — tax, ERP, HRM, CRM, parliament,
school administration. That is the hardest and least glamorous category of
design work, and it is what most B2B companies are actually hiring for. Most
competing portfolios are consumer apps and redesign exercises. This is a
strength being presented as a list of employers.

Draft first lines, to choose or rewrite (open question D):

- "Digital product designer. Five years making government and enterprise
  systems — tax, ERP, HRM — usable by people who have no choice but to use them."
- "Digital product designer in Dhaka. I work on the software people are required
  to use: tax filing, ERP, HR, school administration."
- "Digital product designer. I design complex B2B and government systems —
  20+ shipped products across proptech, govtech and enterprise SaaS."
